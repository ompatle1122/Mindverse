What Is Psychology?

Psychology is the scientific study of the mind and behaviour. It helps us understand how people think, feel, learn, remember, make decisions, and interact with others.

🧠 Thoughts, Feelings & Actions

Imagine you send a message to a friend and they do not reply. Your brain may create different explanations for the same event.

Example:
Thought A: “Maybe they are busy.” You feel calm and continue your day.
Thought B: “Maybe they are angry with me.” You may feel worried and keep checking your phone.

The situation is the same, but your interpretation is different. Thoughts can influence feelings and actions. However, this does not mean every emotion comes only from thoughts.

🔎 Can Psychology Read Minds?

No. Psychology cannot magically tell us what another person is thinking. It studies behaviour and mental processes using research and evidence.

Example: A quiet person might be tired, shy, focused, or simply enjoying some peace. We need more information before deciding why they are quiet.
