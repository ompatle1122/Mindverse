Psychology, in small bites

Quick ideas about the mind, with a little science behind them.

🎯Attention

Your attention is selective

When you're focused on one task, you may miss other things happening nearby. Attention helps us concentrate, but it has limits.

🧩Memory

Remembering takes practice

Trying to recall information from memory—rather than only rereading it—can help strengthen learning. This is often called retrieval practice.

👀Behaviour

First impressions are quick

People can form initial impressions quickly, but a first impression is incomplete and can be wrong. Give people time and context.

🌦️Emotions

Feelings can change

Emotions shift with time, context, sleep, stress, and other factors. One feeling doesn't define your whole personality.

🔁Habits

Small cues can shape habits

What you see around you can remind you to do something. Making a helpful action easy to notice can support building a routine.

💬Communication

Listening helps connection

Paying attention, asking a gentle follow-up, and checking what someone means can reduce misunderstandings in conversations.
